# DeepsGuard360 promotional and explainer video script

**Status:** Narration/script deliverable. This script uses the interactive DeepsGuard360 demo as its visual source. All organizations, people, sites, incidents, locations, and subscription accounts shown in the demo are fictional. GPS markers and CCTV panels are simulations; the live PNG map link is MeshSat (`meshsat.dspng.space`).

## Full explainer (target 3–4 minutes)

### 0:00–0:18 · What it is
**Narration:** What if your entire security operation could run from one connected platform? Meet DeepsGuard360: an integrated, cloud-based Security Management SaaS platform built for security firms and companies in Papua New Guinea and across the Pacific. Each company gets its own private workspace, while Deeps Systems operates the master administration layer.
**Visual:** Demo landing screen and fictional operations summary. On-screen title: “One Platform. Complete Security Operations.”

### 0:18–0:42 · Onboarding and subscription
**Narration:** Start with a guided company registration. Add business and administrator details, simulate email verification, create a private workspace, and choose a plan: Starter for up to 50 guards, Growth for 150, Professional for 300, or Enterprise for a custom workforce.
**Visual:** Walk through Pacific Shield Security Services registration, workspace-ready confirmation, subscription selector, usage and invoice panel.

### 0:42–1:05 · Company dashboard
**Narration:** Company administrators get a clear operational picture: guards, staff on duty, sites, patrols, incidents, contracts, and performance indicators—all in one dashboard.
**Visual:** Dashboard cards, weekly performance chart, and company workspace.

### 1:05–1:31 · Guards and workforce
**Narration:** Manage guard profiles, employee IDs, documents, optional photos, and site assignments. Build weekly rosters, assign shifts, track attendance, and review leave without chasing paper schedules.
**Visual:** Add a fictional guard profile, then review the sample roster and attendance controls.

### 1:31–1:58 · Guard mobile app
**Narration:** In the field, guards can review duties, clock in and out, share a location status, start patrols, scan QR checkpoints, report incidents, and receive control-room messages. Offline mode queues updates for a later sync.
**Visual:** Phone-frame demo. On-screen note: “Device actions are simulated; no real GPS or QR sensor is accessed.”

### 1:58–2:20 · GPS and patrol monitoring
**Narration:** Supervisors get a map-based view of patrol routes, checkpoints, and missed-checkpoint alerts. The demo uses fictional markers; open MeshSat to view the map of Papua New Guinea.
**Visual:** Demo patrol map and direct link to meshsat.dspng.space. Overlay: “Simulated operational markers.”

### 2:20–2:43 · Control room and CCTV
**Narration:** When an alert arrives, the control room can review it, assign a response, and update the status. Camera panels fit into the operational view, while this product demonstration clearly labels CCTV feeds as simulations.
**Visual:** Use the scripted “Attempted break-in at Commercial Site A” scenario, assign response, show simulated camera status.

### 2:43–3:08 · Incident and client management
**Narration:** Capture incident classification, severity, notes, assignment, resolution, and history. Then give clients restricted access to only their assigned sites, attendance, incidents, and monthly reports.
**Visual:** Resolve the fictional incident, then show the Pacific Plaza client portal and report controls.

### 3:08–3:30 · Reporting and multi-company scale
**Narration:** Turn operations into downloadable reports and analytics. At the platform level, manage multiple isolated company workspaces—Pacific Shield, Guardian Protection, and Southern Cross—without mixing their data. DeepsGuard360: one platform, complete security operations. Developed and powered by Deeps Systems.
**Visual:** Report downloads, master admin tenant list, blue DeepsGuard360 end card.

Each security firm gets its own private workspace, with plans for 50 guards, 150, 300, or custom enterprise scale. Built for security companies in Papua New Guinea and the Pacific. DeepsGuard360: one platform, complete security operations.
