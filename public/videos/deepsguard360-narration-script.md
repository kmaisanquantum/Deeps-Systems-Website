# DeepsGuard360 demo narration and production outline

**Status:** Production-ready script/outline; screen recording and final audio/video renders still need to be created. All names, sites, and operational scenarios are fictional demo data.

## Full walkthrough (target 4–5 minutes)

1. **Discovery (0:00–0:18)** — “Meet DeepsGuard360: one connected platform for security operations in Papua New Guinea and across the Pacific. This interactive simulation uses fictional data only.” Show the landing page and features.
2. **Registration (0:18–0:38)** — “A company can register its business and administrator, simulate email verification, create a workspace, and choose a subscription.” Use Pacific Shield Security Services and conclude on the workspace-ready confirmation.
3. **Subscription (0:38–0:58)** — “Select Starter for up to 50 guards, Growth for 150, Professional for 300, or Enterprise for a custom setup. Review usage, invoices, plan changes and renewal.”
4. **Master administration (0:58–1:18)** — “The platform owner manages multiple customer tenants centrally. Pacific Shield, Guardian Protection and Southern Cross each have an isolated workspace.”
5. **Company dashboard (1:18–1:35)** — “Company administrators see guard strength, active shifts, sites, patrols, incidents, contracts and performance at a glance.”
6. **Guard management (1:35–1:53)** — “Maintain guard profiles, employee IDs, documents, optional photos and site assignments.”
7. **Scheduling (1:53–2:10)** — “Build weekly rosters, coordinate shifts, review attendance and track leave.”
8. **Mobile app (2:10–2:34)** — “Guards can review duties, clock in, share a location status, start a patrol, scan checkpoints, submit incidents, receive control room messages, clock out and sync queued updates.”
9. **GPS and patrols (2:34–2:54)** — “Operations teams follow simulated guard and vehicle locations, routes and checkpoints, with attention drawn to a missed checkpoint.”
10. **Control room scenario (2:54–3:18)** — “A simulated alert reports an attempted break-in at Commercial Site A. The controller reviews it, assigns a response and updates its status.”
11. **CCTV (3:18–3:33)** — “Camera status is shown in context. These feed panels are clearly marked simulations, not live surveillance.”
12. **Incident management (3:33–3:53)** — “Capture classification, severity, notes and assignment, then track the response through resolution and history.”
13. **Client portal (3:53–4:13)** — “Clients see only their assigned sites, attendance, incidents and monthly reports—with restricted access by design.”
14. **Reports and scale (4:13–4:40)** — “Download report data, prepare a PDF from the print view, and return to master administration to see how multiple isolated company workspaces fit together. DeepsGuard360: one platform, complete security operations.”
